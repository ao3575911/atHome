# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer, [@ao3575911](https://github.com/ao3575911), through GitHub. Do not use public issues for reports. Reports are kept confidential.
